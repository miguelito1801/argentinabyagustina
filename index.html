About Me

Thinking of a trip or move to Argentina but not sure where to start? Outsource everything to me.

Hello! My name is Agustina Ponce. I was born in Patagonia, spent part of my childhood in Texas, and have been living in Buenos Aires for the last 15 years.

I am fluent in both Spanish and English, have 4 years of experience in the Argentine tourism industry, and hold a BA in hospitality from Gato Duma University.

After years of working in a formal hotel environment, I started this business to personally help tourists, digital nomads, expats, and retirees discover all the wonderful things Argentina has to offer.
