# Welcome, Agustina

This website was created for **Argentina by Agustina** so you can share local recommendations, events and stories without editing code.

From Sanity Studio you can publish articles, places and events, moderate traveler reviews, and update the principal contact information shown on the website.

The public site only displays published content. Traveler reviews remain private until you approve and publish them.

We hope this platform helps you welcome more people to Argentina with the same care and local knowledge you bring to every traveler.
